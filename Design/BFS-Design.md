**Diagonal pathfinding**

With diagonal movement the reachable area becomes larger than with axial movement for the same maximum steps.
More neighbours mean more cells that can be reached, so the search area would be biggger.
A diagonal move could pass between two blocked cells.
A diagonal move is geometrically longer than an axial move, but it would still be counted as one step.
A decision must be made about whether diagonal movement has the same cost as axial movement.
